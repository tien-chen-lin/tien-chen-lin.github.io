Recruitment will begin once a host position is established.</p></header>

<section class="editorial-section">
<div class="section-heading" markdown="0"><p class="eyebrow">The scientific focus</p><h2>A question<br>we can make our own.</h2></div>
<div class="section-body" markdown="1">
The group will begin with two connected questions: when a selected axon becomes difficult to reassign, and when a competing neurite can retain an additional axonal identity. A matched culture–tissue comparison will test how developing tissue changes these outcomes.

The core approach follows the same neurite before, during, and after a reversible perturbation. Quantitative imaging identifies a causal difference; molecular experiments test the mechanism behind it. Projects will be built around a tractable biological question, with a clear first result and room for deeper mechanistic work.

</div>

<section class="editorial-section" id="mentoring">
<div class="section-heading" markdown="0"><p class="eyebrow">Working with me</p><h2>Ownership,<br>feedback, and rigor.</h2></div>
<div class="section-body" markdown="1">
**Project ownership.** My aim is for each researcher to learn to frame a question, design a discriminating experiment, and defend an interpretation. Responsibility and support should develop together.

**Frequent, concrete feedback.** Regular conversations will address the experiment, its interpretation, and what to do next. Feedback on my mentoring is part of that exchange.

**Quantitative skills that travel.** Image analysis, coding, and clear data visualization will be integrated with experimental training. Analysis and methods should be understandable and reusable by someone else.

**Focus and collaboration.** We will pursue mechanisms supported by the data, and bring in specialist expertise when it helps answer a defined question. A broader toolkit should serve the project’s central problem.

**Career development.** Training should support the researcher’s next step, with candid discussion of goals, opportunities, and trade-offs.
</div>
</section>
