### Selected first-author research

- ***[Nature](https://rdcu.be/fsRqy)* 2026** — whole-cell competition between actin networks selects the axon.
- ***J. Cell Biol.* 2016** — reconstitution of an active microtubule-nucleation template.
- ***eLife* 2014** — phosphorylation licenses nucleation in space and time.

These studies combine quantitative perturbation, molecular analysis, and reconstitution. My independent program builds on this foundation to ask how neuronal compartment identities become durable.

**First- and co-corresponding-author review:** [*Trends in Cell Biology*, 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcb.2014.12.002), on γ-tubulin-complex targeting across eukaryotes. This comparative analysis led to the MOZART1 project I independently initiated.
